# Assumptions

The following entries distinguish assumptions and external dependencies from requirements explicitly stated in the assessment. Proposed safeguards are design decisions, not confirmed provider or Salesforce behavior.

| # | Assumption | Why I needed it | Part(s) affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Partial refunds require an explicit business rule before they can change the Salesforce payment status.** Until the rule is confirmed, Quetzal partial refunds are retained with their refunded amount and routed for reconciliation rather than automatically treated as a full `Refunded` payment. | Quetzal supports partial and full refunds, but the supplied `Premium_Payment__c.Status__c` picklist has `Refunded` and no `PartiallyRefunded` value. The effect of a partial refund on policy reinstatement or suspension is unspecified. | **Part A:** canonical event mapping, ordering, Salesforce integration, reconciliation, ADR-0001. **Part B:** Quetzal normalization and contract tests; no Salesforce writes are implemented. **Part D:** exception monitoring and reconciliation procedures. |
| 2 | **For Andina, one `(transaccion.id, transaccion.estado)` pair represents one business transition.** A repeated delivery with a different `notificacion_id` is treated as a retry. Conflicting evidence for the same key is quarantined for investigation. | Andina changes `notificacion_id` between retries and identifies `(transaction ID, state)` as stable. The supplied fields cannot distinguish a genuinely repeated transition to the same state from another retry. | **Part A:** idempotency and event identity. **Part B:** Andina deduplication, webhook tests and canonical publication. **Part D:** conflict investigation and replay. |
| 3 | **Outbound Salesforce traffic uses an enterprise-approved integration gateway or API-management route.** The precise gateway configuration, including whether Mule 4 mediation is mandatory, must be confirmed with Meridian's integration platform team. No third application-owned microservice is assumed. | The scenario prohibits point-to-point system calls and identifies an existing API Manager/integration platform, but does not specify the exact outbound Salesforce routing or mediation topology. | **Part A:** context and sequence diagrams, security boundaries, Salesforce integration, ADR-0001 and ADR-0002. **Part C:** network, identity and gateway configuration. |
| 4 | **The Salesforce Worker is the sole writer of integration-owned payment state, or equivalent Salesforce-side safeguards prevent other writers from overwriting newer state.** Until verified, local per-payment serialization protects only requests controlled by this integration. | The provided Salesforce model exposes a unique external ID and status timestamps, but does not specify conditional-write enforcement or exclusivity against other Salesforce clients. | **Part A:** ordering, idempotency, reconciliation and ADR-0001. **Part C:** integration permissions. **Part D:** stale-write detection and incident recovery. |
| 5 | **The freshness objectives are measured service-level targets, not unconditional guarantees during complete Salesforce outages.** Accepted events remain durable and are processed after recovery; dependency-caused breaches are reported transparently. | The scenario requires 95% visibility within five minutes and 99.5% within one hour, while also documenting Salesforce outages lasting up to 90 minutes. Those conditions can conflict for notifications received during an outage. | **Part A:** capacity and availability analysis. **Part C:** monitoring and scaling. **Part D:** SLO reporting, alerting and recovery. |
| 6 | **Retention periods, database sizing, Worker concurrency limits, batching thresholds, retry delays and operational alert thresholds are initial configuration choices, subject to stakeholder approval and load testing.** They are not values mandated by the provider specifications. | The scenario supplies workload, security and Salesforce API constraints but does not prescribe these operational settings or the applicable country-specific retention schedule. | **Part A:** persistence, capacity, reliability and ADR-0002/0003. **Part B:** configurable retry and test settings where implemented. **Part C:** infrastructure and deployment configuration. **Part D:** operations, retention and performance validation. |

## Verification Plan

- Confirm the business treatment of Quetzal partial refunds with Salesforce and policy owners, including the effect on existing policy automation; until then, keep partial refunds available for reconciliation without triggering an unsupported full-refund state transition.
- Confirm whether Andina can issue multiple genuine transitions with the same transaction ID and state; verify that changed `notificacion_id` values on retries do not create duplicate business events and that `REVERSADA` remains a distinct transition.
- Confirm the approved outbound Salesforce gateway, its authentication and rate-limiting responsibilities, and whether Mule 4 mediation is required.
- Confirm ownership of `Premium_Payment__c` updates and whether Salesforce-side safeguards are needed to prevent competing clients from applying stale states.
- Verify provider HMAC test vectors against exact raw sample bytes, including Quetzal signature rotation, Andina Base64 signatures, and Austral raw-body signing. Use an injectable clock for timestamp-sensitive tests.
- Verify an older `Captured` event cannot overwrite a newer `Refunded` state, including after a Worker crash, uncertain Salesforce response, and replay.
- Verify Salesforce composite upsert partial successes and the documented `INVALID_FIELD`, `UNABLE_TO_LOCK_ROW`, `INVALID_SESSION_ID`, and `REQUEST_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` responses.
- Load-test 40 notifications/second for 15 minutes, 120,000 notifications/day, approximately 3% duplicates, and recovery from 5–90-minute Salesforce outages. Check each provider's acknowledgement deadline, including Andina's restriction to HTTP `200` or `204`.
- Verify authorized operational lookup by provider event ID, payment ID, and Andina delivery-attempt ID; deny unauthorized requests.
- Validate that successful webhook acknowledgements occur only after durable persistence and that retries and replay preserve idempotency and ordering.
- Obtain privacy, security, platform, Salesforce and operations approval for retention, sizing, concurrency, batching and alert thresholds before production rollout.
