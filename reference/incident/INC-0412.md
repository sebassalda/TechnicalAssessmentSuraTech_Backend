## Report

> Since Sunday morning we have **37 cases** from Colombian customers who paid their overdue premium with Andina Pay and whose policies still appear as **suspended**. Customers attach Andina receipts showing the payment as approved. We have no complaints from Mexico or Chile.

## How v1 works

1. Each provider calls its webhook endpoint in the API Manager.
2. An integration application verifies the signature, maps the notification to the canonical event and publishes it to an Azure Service Bus queue per country (`payments-mx`, `payments-co`, `payments-cl`). It then answers `200` to the provider.
3. A consumer per country reads the queue and **upserts** `Premium_Payment__c` in Salesforce, one record per message.
4. Queue settings: lock duration 30 s, **max delivery count 10**. When the Salesforce call fails, the consumer abandons the message and it is redelivered immediately. After 10 deliveries the message goes to the queue's dead-letter queue (DLQ).
